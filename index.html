About Me

I am Daniel Soko, also known publicly as Meta Kiddha, a student and writer from Malawi. I am interested in the questions that shape human life: who we are, what we believe, how we relate to one another and what gives our lives meaning. Through learning, reflection and writing, I hope to contribute ideas that inform, encourage and inspire.
